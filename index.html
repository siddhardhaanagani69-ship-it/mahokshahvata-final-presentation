One website, six things you can do

Market

Search 4,000 US stocks. 90-day charts. "Explain this stock" in plain English.

GET /momentum

Portfolio

Buy and sell by shares or by dollars. Live profit and loss.

POST /portfolio/{id}/trade-stock

Sentiment

Live news headlines, tagged with the stocks they name.

GET /news-sentiment

Options

Buy and close calls and puts, with a cost preview.

POST /portfolio/{id}/trade-option

Autopilot

Trades on momentum rules, on a schedule or on demand.

Email and password, or Google. A profile photo.

Security, layer by layer

In transit
HTTPS only, with a Google-managed certificate. Plain http gets a 301 redirect.
Sign-in
scrypt with a random salt. One message for a wrong email or password. Repeated failures: blocked up to 15 min.
Secrets · least privilege
Secret Manager, held in memory only. Pods use a service account; GitHub uses Workload Identity Federation. Non-root container.
Data
Cloud SQL has no public IP and accepts only encrypted connections. Private bucket; photos checked by their bytes.
